
## Vanity Metrics vs. Actionable KPIs
Getting a bunch of likes feels great, but it does not always mean your business is growing. Likes are "vanity metrics"—they look nice on the surface, yet people often double-tap without really paying attention. Actionable KPIs like saves, shares, and click-through rates tell a much better story because they show someone found your post helpful enough to keep, send to a friend, or visit your website.

## Measuring Engagement and Reach
To see if your content is truly connecting, look beyond basic view counts. Pay attention to comment sentiment during your daily [[community-management]] to see whether people are excited, confused, or frustrated by what you share. For [[short-form-video]], check your average view duration to see how many seconds people actually watch before swiping away, which tells you if your hook is working.

## Monthly Reporting Checklist
At the end of every month, set aside time to pull your numbers together and see what worked. Look at your top-performing posts, compare follower growth, and check how many people clicked through to your site. Reviewing these simple stats helps you spot clear patterns so you can double down on winning ideas and fix what fell flat next month.
