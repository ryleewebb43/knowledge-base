
## The Rise of Vertical Video
Quick, vertical videos have completely changed how people scroll on their phones. Platforms like TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts make it easy to watch dozens of bite-sized clips in just a few minutes. Because these apps show videos based on what people like rather than just who they follow, even small accounts can reach huge audiences quickly with a single entertaining or helpful clip.

## Anatomy of a High-Performing Clip
A great short video starts with a strong hook in the first couple of seconds so viewers do not swipe away. Fast pacing, quick scene changes, and clear on-screen captions keep people watching all the way through—especially when they are scrolling with the sound off. Planning these clips ahead of time inside your [[content-calenders]] helps you stay consistent and tie your quick videos into bigger promotions.

## Best Practices for Retention
1. Hook viewers in the first 3 seconds
2. Use on-screen captions for silent viewers
3. End with a clear call to action

> "Attention is the currency of social media; if you don't earn it in the first three seconds, the rest of the video doesn't matter."

## Collaborating with Creators
Making great videos every day takes a lot of time, so brands often hire outside talent to film clips for them through [[inluencer-partnerships]]. Everyday creators already know what trends work best and how to talk naturally on camera. Teaming up with them gives your brand relatable, genuine videos that feel like a friendly recommendation rather than a stiff, traditional commercial.
